# Reckon HRMS Lite: simple HR, attendance and salary payment

Reckon HRMS Lite is a simple HR application from Reckon Technologies Ltd. for Frappe and ERPNext v16. The idea is that an everyday HR user should never need to understand salary structures, payroll entries or ledger postings. They manage people, attendance and pay in plain screens, and the accounting happens behind the scenes. It is part of the ReckonERP offering at [app.reckon.tech](https://app.reckon.tech).

## Detailed features

### Employees
- Employee records on the standard Frappe HR employee, with a gross salary field for simple pay setup
- One place for the people data that attendance and salary payment use

### Attendance
- Attendance and check-ins from Frappe HR
- Attendance is processed automatically each day, so month-end does not start with manual counting

### Salary payment
- Per-employee processing with editable gross pay, allowances, bonuses and deductions
- Bulk salary processing: pay all employees at once with editable columns
- No need to learn salary structures or payroll entries

### Accounting built in
- Journal and payment entries from native ERPNext accounting
- Entries are posted automatically when salary is paid

### Payslips
- Professional printable PDF payslip for each payment

### Reports and dashboard
- Reports for attendance, salary, leave, late arrivals, deductions, allowances, history and paid salary
- A Reckon HRMS workspace with number cards and analytics

## Built on Frappe HR and ERPNext

Frappe HR supplies employee and attendance records and ERPNext supplies the accounting, so pay flows into proper books. Reckon adds the simple screens, bulk salary run, payslips and reports on top.

## Who it is for

Small and mid-size businesses in Bangladesh that run salary from spreadsheets today and want attendance, pay and accounting connected without a heavy payroll setup.
